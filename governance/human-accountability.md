# Human Accountability Framework

### Core Principle
AI prepares or assists. A qualified human remains accountable for consequential decisions.

### Permitted AI Activities
The AI workflows in this repository are authorized to:
* Extract and classify information
* Summarize documents
* Structure unstructured data
* Propose classifications
* Identify uncertainty or missing information
* Recommend operational routing

### Mandatory Human Activities
Human professionals MUST:
* Validate AI-extracted facts
* Interpret legal and regulatory applicability
* Approve or reject recommendations
* Determine operational materiality
* Accept ownership of the workflow
* Authorise any material escalation
* Approve remediation steps
* Act on and close the decision

The human boundary is built into the workflow, not added as a generic disclaimer.
