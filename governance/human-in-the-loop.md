# Human-in-the-Loop

## Principle

AI prepares or assists. A qualified human remains accountable for consequential decisions.

### AI
- extracts;
- classifies;
- summarises;
- proposes;
- flags uncertainty.

### Human
- validates;
- interprets;
- approves;
- rejects;
- escalates;
- owns the decision.

The boundary must be explicit in the workflow rather than implied by a disclaimer.
