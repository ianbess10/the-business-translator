# The Business Translator Lens

| Question | Purpose |
|---|---|
| What business problem are we solving? | Prevent technology-first thinking |
| What decision or activity changes? | Establish business relevance |
| What information does AI need? | Identify context requirements |
| What could AI get wrong? | Identify operational risk |
| How will we measure performance? | Establish evidence |
| Where must humans intervene? | Establish accountability |
| What would make this safe to scale? | Establish governance |

## Decision rule

An AI capability is not considered successful because the output looks plausible.

It is successful only when its performance is measurable, its limitations are understood, and its role in the business process is controlled.
