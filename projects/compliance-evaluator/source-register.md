# Official Source Register

**Scope:** South African AML/CFT and FSCA market conduct

**Verified:** 9 August 2026

**Purpose:** authoritative-source boundary for Project 2 design

This register records the approved discovery sources for the project. It does not convert every source into an applicable obligation. Instrument status, effective date, amendments, applicability and authoritative text must be confirmed during corpus preparation and human review.

## Admission rules

A source may enter the evaluation corpus only when its record includes:

1. issuing authority;
2. official title and source URL;
3. instrument type and legal/status classification;
4. publication, effective and relevant transition dates where applicable;
5. version or amendment position;
6. applicability conditions;
7. supersession or withdrawal check;
8. approved source excerpt and locator; and
9. human reviewer approval.

## Refresh triggers

Re-verify the register when:

- the FIC or FSCA publishes a new or amended instrument;
- a draft becomes final, is withdrawn or is replaced;
- commencement or transition dates change;
- the synthetic institution profile changes;
- a new licence, product, entity or distribution channel enters scope; or
- an evaluation run is about to be represented as current.

Official-source verification does not itself establish how an instrument applies to any real institution.
