# Evidence-Gated Decision Pipeline v1.2 — Decision Record

## Decision

**Proceed later with a targeted, separately versioned v1.2 implementation. Do not modify, tune or rerun v1.1.**

This record selects an architecture and its operating controls. It does not implement v1.2, create a v1.2 prompt or workflow directory, change the frozen labels, execute a model or approve holdout preparation.

## Why a new version is warranted

v1.1 improved end-to-end exactness from 62.5% to 83.3%, made applicability exact and achieved perfect escalation precision and recall on the frozen 24-case regression set. Its remaining decision errors are concentrated and causally coherent:

- one source-backed candidate obligation was rejected because legal readiness contaminated statement support;
- two complete mappings were demoted because missing evidence contaminated coverage; and
- one partial submitted mapping was silently completed by importing an additional catalogue control.

A fourth diagnostic issue sits in the evaluator rather than the workflow: a legitimate policy rule ID collided with a benchmark case ID. These failures justify stronger stage and identity boundaries. They do not justify prompt-only tuning, case-specific exceptions, label changes or weaker escalation controls.

## Selected v1.2 architecture

### 1. Source support and legal readiness become separate decisions

For cases without an approved upstream obligation, v1.2 must distinguish:

1. whether the candidate statement is supported by the supplied source wording; and
2. whether commencement, source-chain or human-approval conditions prevent operational approval.

A statement that faithfully reproduces the supplied source cannot be labelled an unsupported extension merely because commencement is staged or human approval is still required. Legal readiness may keep the obligation at candidate status and keep assurance closed without changing factual source support.

The source-status and applicability controls that passed remain authoritative.

### 2. Mapping and evidence use isolated model stages

The combined v1.1 control/evidence model stage will be replaced by two bounded stages for entered assurance:

**Mapping stage**

- receives the approved atomic obligation, current proposed mapping and relevant catalogue controls;
- does not receive presented-evidence status, evidence-gap proposals or escalation proposals;
- decides current mapping membership and uncovered obligation elements; and
- may identify additional catalogue controls only as remediation candidates.

**Evidence stage**

- receives the approved obligation, the validated current mapping and presented evidence;
- does not decide mapping membership or add controls;
- assesses design documentation, missing operating evidence and adverse operating evidence; and
- does not decide escalation or compliance.

This input isolation is selected because one combined response with separate fields did not reliably create independent decisions.

### 3. Current controls and remediation candidates are different records

The v1.2 mapping contract must distinguish:

- `current_mapping_control_ids` — controls actually present in the submitted mapping and validated against the catalogue; and
- `candidate_additional_control_ids` — relevant catalogue controls that may close identified coverage but are not part of the current operating state.

Additional candidates must never be inserted automatically into the current mapping. Their owners may be displayed for a mapping review, but they cannot make the present mapping complete or evidenced.

### 4. Generality measurement becomes identity-safe

The versioned v1.2 evaluator must not infer a case-specific override from substring overlap between a case ID and a legitimate policy rule ID.

The generality gate will require:

- an explicit structured `case_specific_override: false` trace;
- a static check that executable workflow artefacts contain no benchmark-label access or case-targeted branches; and
- structured rule-identity comparison rather than free-text substring matching.

The frozen v1.1 evaluation remains the official v1.1 score. Correcting this evaluator defect in v1.2 must not be described as a model-performance improvement.

## Expected execution shape

If implemented, v1.2 is expected to retain 16 source/obligation calls for cases without an approved obligation and use two isolated calls for each of the eight entered-assurance cases:

- 16 source/support assessments;
- 8 current-mapping assessments; and
- 8 evidence assessments.

The planned regression total is therefore **32 model calls**. This additional cost is accepted because it creates an auditable operating boundary between coverage and evidence. The exact execution design must be confirmed and frozen during implementation.

## Controls retained without relaxation

v1.2 will retain:

- deterministic source-transition policy;
- reviewed approved-obligation authority and source-stage bypass;
- fact-based applicability and the approved applicability boundary;
- catalogue-based owner resolution;
- distinct sufficient-evidence, missing-evidence, design-gap and operating-exception outcomes;
- partial-coverage treatment without default escalation;
- all mandatory escalation triggers and accountable roles;
- cross-field reconciliation;
- mandatory human review and `not_determined` compliance conclusion;
- runner isolation from evaluator-only labels; and
- no mock fallback, overwrite, post-result tuning or rerun.

The frozen regression dataset and model snapshot remain unchanged during the future v1.2 regression so the workflow change can be isolated.

## Prohibited v1.2 responses

The future implementation must not:

- edit any v1.1 workflow, result or analysis artefact;
- rerun or rescore v1.1;
- encode case IDs, expected labels or case-specific wording in executable artefacts;
- change frozen regression labels after observing v1.1;
- let evidence status determine mapping completeness;
- add a remediation-candidate control to the current mapping;
- treat staged commencement or pending approval as proof that source wording is unsupported;
- weaken escalation rules that achieved zero false positives and zero false negatives; or
- combine regression improvement with independent-validation or production claims.

## v1.2 acceptance conditions

Before regression, v1.2 must have offline boundary tests proving:

1. source-statement support remains separate from commencement and approval readiness;
2. mapping-stage inputs contain no presented-evidence status or escalation proposal;
3. evidence-stage outputs cannot change mapping membership;
4. current mapping controls are restricted to the submitted mapping;
5. additional controls remain remediation candidates only;
6. missing or adverse evidence cannot create a coverage gap;
7. catalogue owners resolve for both current and candidate controls without changing membership;
8. the generality gate accepts legitimate rule IDs that overlap syntactically with case IDs; and
9. no executable workflow artefact reads evaluator labels or implements case-specific branches.

The versioned regression evaluator must retain the operating release gates and correct only the defective identity check. All gates remain mandatory. Passing one future frozen regression permits unseen-holdout preparation; it does not establish production readiness.
