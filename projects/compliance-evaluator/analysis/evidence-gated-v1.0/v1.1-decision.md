# Evidence-Gated Decision Pipeline v1.1 — Decision Record

## Decision

**Proceed with a targeted v1.1 implementation. Do not modify or rerun v1.0.**

This is a versioned workflow decision, not a release approval. v1.1 must be implemented in a new workflow directory, tested offline and frozen before one regression execution.

## Why a new version is warranted

v1.0 passed seven of eight operating release gates and improved end-to-end exactness by 58.3 percentage points over the simple baseline. Its remaining failures are concentrated and causally coherent:

- applicability was wrong in all nine failed cases;
- mapping completeness was conflated with evidence or operating performance in five cases; and
- one partial mapping was misclassified as a high design deficiency, creating the sole unnecessary escalation.

These are correctable workflow-boundary issues. They do not justify changing the frozen labels, reducing human accountability or weakening the escalation rules that detected all six mandatory escalations.

## Selected v1.1 changes

### 1. Approved-obligation authority boundary

For an assurance record with a supplied, human-approved upstream obligation:

- the control-assurance path must not reopen source authority or approved obligation status;
- applicability must be derived from the approved obligation and explicit institution facts;
- absent an explicit material applicability fact, source conflict or reviewed out-of-scope fact, applicability remains `applicable_candidate`; and
- source-stage narrative about commencement or legal effect cannot silently change the approved assurance record.

Regulatory-change cases without an approved upstream obligation will continue to use the existing source and applicability assessment path.

### 2. Orthogonal mapping and evidence decisions

The v1.1 stage contract must answer two independent questions:

1. **Mapping completeness:** do the supported controls collectively cover the approved obligation?
2. **Evidence assessment:** what does the supplied evidence establish about design and operation?

The following invariants will be enforced:

- missing operating evidence does not make a complete mapping partial;
- adverse operating evidence does not make a complete mapping partial;
- sufficient evidence does not make a partial mapping complete; and
- applicability or source commencement reasoning does not change mapping completeness.

### 3. Partial-coverage precedence

Where supported controls cover only part of an obligation and no explicit evidence establishes an inadequate control design:

- `mapping_status = partially_mapped`;
- `assurance_outcome = potential_control_gap`;
- `gap_types = [partial_coverage]`;
- `gap_severity = medium`;
- `remediation_action_types = [mapping_review]`; and
- no escalation is required by default.

`control_design` and its high-severity escalation trigger remain reserved for evidence that supports an actual design deficiency.

## Controls retained without relaxation

v1.1 will retain:

- the frozen source-transition table and deterministic final source disposition;
- the human-approved assurance-entry gate and downstream normalisation;
- catalogue-based control-owner resolution;
- distinct missing-evidence, design-gap and operating-exception treatments;
- all four mandatory escalation triggers and their accountable roles;
- cross-field reconciliation;
- mandatory human review and `not_determined` compliance conclusion;
- input-only runner isolation from evaluator labels; and
- overwrite, mock fallback and post-observation rerun prohibitions.

The model snapshot remains `gpt-4o-mini-2024-07-18`, temperature remains `0`, and the frozen 24-case regression set remains unchanged so the effect of the workflow change can be isolated.

## Prohibited v1.1 responses

The implementation must not:

- encode case IDs, expected labels or benchmark-specific wording;
- change the v1.0 workflow, results or analysis;
- alter the frozen regression labels after observing v1.0;
- lower escalation sensitivity to eliminate the false positive;
- treat an approved obligation as proof that its control is effective;
- combine regression improvement with independent-validation claims; or
- run v1.1 before its prompt, schemas, policies, tests, evaluator and hashes are committed.

## v1.1 regression acceptance gates

The original eight hard gates remain mandatory. v1.1 adds explicit diagnostic gates:

1. applicability is exact across all 24 regression cases;
2. mapping status is exact across all eight entered assurance cases;
3. evidence outcome, gap type, severity and remediation are exact across all eight entered cases;
4. `CON-010`-type partial coverage does not become a design deficiency or escalation;
5. missing or adverse operating evidence does not demote an otherwise complete mapping; and
6. every corrected decision is attributable to a general rule rather than a case-specific exception.

End-to-end exactness must still be reported. Passing regression gates will permit holdout preparation, not a production-readiness claim.
