# Synthetic South African Investment & Wealth Institution

## Profile identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Profile ID | `SA-IWM-SYN-001` |
| Version | `1.0` |
| Status | frozen for baseline design |
| Jurisdiction | South Africa |
| Institution type | Fictional investment and wealth-management institution |

This is a controlled evaluation profile. It is not a real institution and does not establish the legal or regulatory applicability of any requirement to any real entity.

## Business model

The institution serves retail and affluent customers through:

- investment advice;
- discretionary portfolio management;
- investment-account administration;
- portfolio reporting; and
- client servicing and complaints handling.

Its synthetic product set includes collective investment schemes, exchange-traded funds, listed equities, government and corporate bonds, and money-market instruments.

Customer relationships are managed through employed financial advisers, a digital portal, a client-service centre and approved third-party intermediaries. Execution, custody and selected administration activities are outsourced, while the institution retains customer, control and oversight accountability within the fictional operating model.

## Regulatory assumptions

For evaluation only:

- the institution is assumed to fall within an applicable FIC Act accountable-institution category; and
- it is assumed to hold the FSCA authorisations and permissions required for the supplied advice, intermediary and discretionary-management activities.

These are scenario assumptions, not legal conclusions. Exact classifications, exclusions, permissions, effective dates and instrument applicability must be determined from the approved source pack by an authorised human reviewer.

If an obligation depends on a fact not stated in this profile, the capability must return `applicability_uncertain`.

## Customers and risk features

The profile covers:

- retail and affluent natural persons;
- family and inter vivos trusts;
- owner-managed private companies;
- non-resident customers;
- foreign beneficial owners;
- domestic or foreign politically exposed persons;
- higher-risk geographic exposure;
- complex ownership structures; and
- cross-border funding or withdrawals.

Anonymous accounts, cash deposits or withdrawals, and virtual-asset services are excluded.

## AML/CFT processes

1. AML/CFT risk assessment and RMCP governance
2. Customer onboarding and due diligence
3. Beneficial-ownership identification and verification
4. Screening, customer risk rating and enhanced review
5. Ongoing monitoring and customer-information refresh
6. Transaction surveillance and internal escalation
7. Regulatory reporting and record keeping

## Market-conduct processes

1. Product and service approval
2. Advice, suitability and mandate management
3. Customer disclosure, fees and communications
4. Order placement, execution oversight and conflicts management
5. Customer servicing, complaints and remediation
6. Customer-outcomes monitoring and management information

The catalogue describes possible control mappings. A control is not treated as effective merely because it appears in the profile. Evaluation cases must supply the evidence required to assess it.

## Evidence catalogue

Candidate evidence includes approved source metadata, impact assessments, policies, RMCP sections, procedures, system rules, completed control records, escalation records, regulatory acknowledgements, training records, management information, governance minutes, testing results and remediation evidence.

Missing operating evidence must produce `insufficient_evidence` or `potential_control_gap` as supported by the case—not a compliance conclusion.

## Explicit exclusions

Release 1 excludes:

- deposit-taking and prudential banking regulation;
- credit provision;
- insurance underwriting;
- retirement-fund trusteeship or administration;
- virtual assets;
- market-infrastructure operation;
- proprietary trading;
- tax advice;
- legal opinions; and
- real customer, employee, transaction or institution data.

## Evaluation boundary

The capability must:

1. use only the frozen profile and approved source pack;
2. expose missing applicability facts;
3. distinguish binding sources, guidance, plans, drafts and superseded material;
4. cite every obligation and mapping;
5. preserve human decision authority; and
6. avoid `compliant` and `non_compliant` outcomes.

Changes to the institution scope require a new profile version.
