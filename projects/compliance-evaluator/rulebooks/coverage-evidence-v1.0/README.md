# Coverage and Evidence Rulebook v1.0

This human-approved synthetic rulebook converts one approved complaints-framework obligation into stable obligation elements, control-coverage assertions and typed evidence requirements.

It was authored from the approved obligation record, its source locator and the frozen control catalogue. It deliberately does not reproduce benchmark case IDs, evidence IDs, evaluator labels or case-specific exceptions.

The rulebook treats complaint handling (`C-CON-006`) and customer-outcomes oversight (`C-CON-007`) as complementary controls covering separate mandatory elements. Both are required for complete coverage of the full approved obligation. Missing authority must quarantine rather than invite model inference.

Approval is synthetic and educational. It is not legal advice, a compliance opinion or production control documentation.
