# Pilot Operating Authority

| Activity | Prepares | Decides/approves | Independent challenge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source intake and metadata | Regulatory change analyst | Regulatory Change Owner | Compliance assurance |
| Legal status and interpretation | AI may structure supplied facts | Qualified legal/compliance authority | Independent legal/compliance reviewer where required |
| Applicability | AI may identify questions | Accountable compliance adviser with business facts | Business and legal challenge |
| Obligation statement | AI may draft from approved locator | Qualified compliance/legal reviewer | Regulatory Change Owner |
| Control mapping | AI may propose candidates | Control Framework Owner and control owner | Compliance monitoring |
| Evidence sufficiency | AI may classify bounded observations | Qualified assurance reviewer | Independent assurance sample |
| Severity and escalation | Approved policy derives advice | Accountable compliance authority | Pilot Executive for disputes |
| Remediation | AI may propose routing | Control owner and compliance authority | Risk or assurance as required |
| Record update or closure | No AI authority | Existing authorised operating process | Audit trail review |
| Pilot continuation | Pilot team recommends | Pilot Executive | Independent Assurance and AI/Model Risk |

## Separation requirements

- The analyst producing the current-process baseline must not see AI output before locking that baseline record.
- The qualified reviewer may not be the sole preparer of both comparison records.
- The person approving a remediation or closure must have the same or stronger authority required by the existing process.
- Repository authorship, workflow development and benchmark evaluation do not confer institutional decision authority.
- Pilot metric owners may calculate results but may not remove failed or disputed work items from the denominator.
