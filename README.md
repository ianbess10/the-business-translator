# My Prompt Engineering Method

**Understand → Baseline → Design → Test → Evaluate → Improve → Govern → Operationalise**

Every case study documents the progression from an intentionally weak baseline to an engineered workflow.

The portfolio distinguishes between:
- model capability;
- prompt quality;
- evaluation performance;
- business usefulness; and
- readiness for controlled operational use.

# Responsible AI

> **AI assists. Professionals remain accountable.**

The portfolio uses:
- synthetic or appropriately controlled test data;
- source grounding;
- citation requirements;
- explicit uncertainty;
- human review;
- auditability;
- version-controlled prompts;
- defined escalation conditions.

Regulatory, investment and compliance decisions remain subject to appropriate professional authority.

---

# The Business Translator Lens

For every AI opportunity:

1. What business problem are we solving?
2. What decision or activity changes?
3. What information does AI need?
4. What could AI get wrong?
5. How will performance be measured?
6. Where must humans intervene?
7. What would make this safe to scale?

The objective is not to make an AI produce impressive text.

The objective is to determine:

> **Where can AI create value, how can that value be measured, what can go wrong, and what controls are required before the capability can be trusted?**
